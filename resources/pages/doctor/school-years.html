School Years

Records start fresh every school year. Past years stay on file and can always be opened.

A visit belongs to the school year its date falls in, so changing a school year’s dates moves its visits with it. Visits dated after a school year’s end date and before the next one starts (the summer break) stay with the year that just ended. When a school year ends, the next one is added automatically with the same dates one year later — edit it here when the real dates are announced.
